What sets this apart

This isn't a stat sheet. It's a mirror.

Most trading journals hand you forty metrics and call it insight — win rate, average R, Sharpe ratio, numbers on top of numbers. Most traders check them once, feel good or bad for five minutes, and go back to trading exactly the way they always have.

Accountatrade does something different. After a stretch of trades, it doesn't just show you what happened — it reflects it back to you the way an honest mentor would say it out loud. Not a score. Not a grade. A question, or a pattern, put in front of you so you can actually see it. Not how much you made — what you did, and what you could have done instead.

Beyond P&L

A bad day doesn't erase good habits.

Accountatrade never tells you that you failed — it asks what happened, and what you'd try differently instead. A losing streak with a solid process is still progress, and that's the part it keeps track of.
